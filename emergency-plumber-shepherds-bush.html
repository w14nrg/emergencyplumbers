Terraces, estates and new builds

W12 covers Shepherd's Bush Green, the streets off the Uxbridge Road, Goldhawk Road and Askew Road, the White City and Wormholt estates, and the new developments around White City.

  • Victorian terraces: many split into flats, with shared supplies and stacks, and some original lead or iron supply pipe.
  • Large estates: purpose-built blocks where communal pipework and some valves are managed by the council or housing association.
  • New-build towers: plastic push-fit pipework, booster pumps and building management teams.

Emergencies we see in Shepherd's Bush

We expect leaks between flats in converted terraces, push-fit fittings coming apart in newer flats, blocked toilets and wastes, and seized stopcocks in older houses to be the most common emergencies in W12. In a council or housing association flat, a communal pipe or stack may be the landlord's responsibility — call their emergency repairs line too, but don't wait if water is coming in.

Getting to you

For new-build developments, tell us about concierge, fobs and car-park access. For estates, tell us which block and entrance. In terraced streets, let us know if the flat above or below shares your supply.

Coverage

W12 is in our core area, which means it's one of the areas we can reach most reliably out of hours. When you contact us we'll give you a realistic arrival time for that night — we don't quote fixed response times on the website because they depend on traffic, time and what else is going on.
