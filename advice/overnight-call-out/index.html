What happens on an overnight plumbing call-out

If you've never called a plumber at 3am, it helps to know what to expect — from the first message to the plumber leaving.

WhatsApp an emergencyCall now

1. The first message

We'll ask for your postcode, what's happening, whether water is still escaping and whether you've turned it off. A photo or video makes a big difference. If water is pouring, call rather than message.

2. Time and price

We reply with a realistic arrival estimate and the charge for the overnight band. Nothing is booked until you say yes. If we think it can safely wait until morning, we'll tell you — a daytime visit costs less.

3. While we're on the way

We'll talk you through anything useful: finding the stopcock, draining the pipes, protecting the ceiling below. Clear a path to the leak and the stopcock, and arrange access to any communal areas.

4. On arrival

The plumber checks the water is off — or isolates it — then finds the cause. We protect floors and finishes, and open up as little as possible.

5. The repair

Many repairs are done on the spot: replacing a section of pipe, a failed fitting, a hose or a valve; clearing a blocked toilet or waste. If a specific part isn't available at night, we isolate the fault and, where possible, restore water and heating to the rest of the property.

6. Before we leave

We test everything we've touched, explain what failed and why, and agree any follow-up. You'll get a written record of the work and the charge, which is useful for insurance.

Need someone now?

Send your postcode and a photo, or call. We'll tell you honestly how soon we can get there and what it will cost.
