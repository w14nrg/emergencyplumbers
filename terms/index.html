Service information

Service terms

A short, plain-English summary of how our emergency service works. We'll confirm the key points with you in writing before we attend.

Before we attend

  • We tell you the charge for your time band, and how time after the first hour is charged, before anyone sets off. Nothing is booked until you agree.
  • Arrival times are estimates based on the information at the time. We'll keep you updated if anything changes.

On the visit

  • Our first priority is making the situation safe, which may mean isolating water to part or all of the property.
  • We agree any parts with you before fitting them.
  • If a full repair isn't possible on the visit — because a part isn't available at night or the fault needs more extensive work — we'll explain the options and any cost before arranging a return visit.
  • We don't carry out gas work or electrical work.

Access

Please make sure we can reach the problem and the stopcock, and arrange access to communal areas or neighbouring flats where needed.

Payment

We'll confirm how to pay when we confirm the visit. You'll receive a written record of the work and charges.

Cancelling

If you no longer need us, please tell us as soon as possible. If you cancel once we're already on the way, we'll explain any charge before you decide.
