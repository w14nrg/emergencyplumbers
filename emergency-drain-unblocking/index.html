Blocked sinks and wastes

Blocked sink, bath and waste pipes

A blocked kitchen sink or a shower tray that won't empty becomes urgent fast — especially if it's backing up into another fitting or leaking from the waste.

Do this now

  1. Stop running water into the blocked fitting.
  2. If a dishwasher or washing machine drains into the same waste, switch it off mid-cycle.
  3. Check the trap under the sink or basin — if you're comfortable, put a bowl underneath and unscrew it to clear it.
  4. Try a plunger, covering the overflow hole with a damp cloth so it can build pressure.
  5. Avoid caustic drain cleaners if the fitting is full of standing water.

When a blocked waste is an emergency

A slow basin can wait. It's urgent when:

  • The kitchen sink is full and you can't use it, or it's backing up into the dishwasher.
  • A shower or bath won't empty and water is overflowing onto the floor.
  • Water is leaking from the waste pipe under the fitting or into a ceiling below.
  • Several fittings are backing up at once — which usually means a blockage further down the system.

Common causes

Kitchen sinks

Fat and grease that set in the trap and pipe, food waste, and coffee grounds. Often worse where the waste pipe has a long, shallow run to the stack.

Basins, baths and showers

Hair and soap building up in the trap, and in pop-up wastes and shower traps that are hard to reach.

Shared waste pipes

Where a kitchen and bathroom join one waste pipe, a blockage in one fitting can back up into the other.

Washing machines

Lint and fluff in the standpipe or trap, or a kinked drain hose, causing water to back up into the sink.

How we clear it

  1. Find where it is. We check which fittings are affected to work out whether the blockage is in a trap, a branch pipe or the main stack.
  2. Strip and clean the trap and waste. Most sink and basin blockages are in the first metre of pipe.
  3. Use a hand drain auger. For blockages further along a waste pipe, a flexible auger reaches through the pipe without dismantling it.
  4. Re-make leaking joints. If the blockage has caused a waste joint to leak, we replace the seal or fitting.

If the blockage turns out to be in the underground drain and needs jetting or a camera survey, we'll tell you straight away and explain who to contact, rather than charging you to try.

Questions

Are drain unblocking chemicals safe to use?

On a slow drain, an enzyme cleaner is fine. Avoid caustic products in a fitting full of standing water, and never mix products. Tell us what you've used before we start.

Why does my kitchen sink smell?

Usually food and grease in the trap, or a trap that has dried out. If you can also smell sewage elsewhere, there may be a problem with the drain or a failed seal.
