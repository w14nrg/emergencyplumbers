Contact

Contact us

WhatsApp is quickest — send your postcode, what's happening and a photo. If water is pouring and you can't stop it, call.

Contact buttons appear once the new number is added

What to send us

  1. Your postcode.
  2. What's happening, in a sentence — "pipe burst under the kitchen sink", "water coming through the bedroom ceiling".
  3. Whether water is still escaping, and whether you've managed to turn it off.
  4. A photo or short video of the problem, and of the stopcock if you can find it.
  5. Anything about access: flat number, porter, gate codes, parking.

WhatsApp messages from our website start with a short template to fill in, but they can't attach photos for you — please add them to the chat yourself.

Gas or electrics?

If you can smell gas, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. For a power cut or damaged electricity supply, call 105.

Need someone now?

Send your postcode and a photo, or call. We'll tell you honestly how soon we can get there and what it will cost.
