Out of hours

Out-of-hours plumber in London

Late at night, at the weekend or on a bank holiday, you want two things: someone who answers, and an honest idea of when they'll arrive and what it will cost.

Do this now

  1. If water is escaping, turn off the stopcock first and open the cold taps.
  2. Message us your postcode, what's happening and a photo — or call if it's pouring.
  3. Tell us whether water is still coming out, and whether it's reaching another flat.
  4. Clear a path to the leak and the stopcock so we can get straight to work.

How a night call-out works

  1. You contact us. WhatsApp is quickest because a photo tells us so much. If water is pouring and you can't stop it, call instead.
  2. We reply with timing and the charge. You'll get a realistic arrival estimate for your postcode and the charge for that time band. Nothing is booked until you agree.
  3. We make it safe. First job is always stopping the water: isolating the pipe, the cylinder or the whole supply.
  4. We fix what can be fixed safely tonight. Most leaks, failed fittings and blockages are repaired on the visit. If something needs parts that aren't available at night, we leave it isolated and safe, and explain the follow-up.

What's worth a night call-out?

Night and bank holiday time costs more, so it should be spent on problems that genuinely can't wait. Call us out of hours for water you can't stop, water coming through a ceiling, a blocked only toilet, a stopcock that won't close, or a leaking cylinder or tank. A drip you can isolate at a valve, or a second toilet that's blocked, can usually wait until a daytime visit. Send a photo and we'll tell you honestly which it is.

Weekends, Sundays and bank holidays

We take enquiries every day of the year, including Sundays, Christmas and Easter. Bank holidays are often when things go wrong — guests, extra showers and baths, and heating left on for long periods all put extra strain on old pipework and wastes.

What it costs

Charges are set in time bands — weekday daytime, evenings and weekends, overnight, and bank holidays — with a first-hour charge and then half-hourly time. We tell you the charge for your time of day before anyone sets off, and any parts are agreed before they're fitted. How our pricing works.

What we can and can't do at night

Most emergency repairs use standard copper and plastic fittings, valves and waste parts that we can carry. The exceptions are specific parts — a particular cylinder, an unusual concealed shower valve, a bespoke radiator — which may need ordering. In those cases we isolate the fault so the rest of your water and heating work, and come back once the part is in.

In a flat or mansion block?

If the leak is in a communal area, a shared riser or a flat you can't get into, tell us when you message. At night we may need the porter, the managing agent's out-of-hours line or a neighbour to give access. We'll tell you what we need so you can arrange it while we're on the way.

Need someone now?

Send your postcode and a photo, or call. We'll tell you honestly how soon we can get there and what it will cost.
