Prices

Emergency plumber prices

You'll know what you're paying before we set off. Here's how emergency charges are worked out, and what affects the final bill.

Charges by time band

Our current rates for each time band are confirmed with you before anyone sets off. Send your postcode and the time you need us, and we'll reply with the exact charge.

The first-hour charge covers getting to you, finding the problem and the first hour of work. After that, time is charged in half hours. Many emergency visits — isolating a burst, replacing a failed fitting, clearing a blocked WC — are finished within the first hour or two.

What's included, and what isn't

  • Included: travel to you, diagnosis, the time worked, and standard consumables such as jointing compound and PTFE tape.
  • Parts: fittings, valves, pipe and replacement parts are charged on top, and we agree them with you before they're fitted.
  • Parking: where we can't park legally for free, we'll tell you in advance if a parking cost will apply.

Why we don't advertise "no call-out fee"

Some firms advertise no call-out fee and recover it through a higher hourly rate or a long minimum charge. Others show a low headline price that only applies in office hours. We'd rather tell you the real charge for your time of night up front, so you can decide whether it's worth it or whether the problem can wait until morning.

When a problem can wait

If your problem can be safely isolated — a dripping tap with its own valve, a second toilet when another works — a daytime visit will cost less. Send a photo and we'll tell you honestly if it's worth waiting.

Paying

We'll explain how to pay when we confirm the visit. You'll get a written record of the work done and the charge.

Need someone now?

Send your postcode and a photo, or call. We'll tell you honestly how soon we can get there and what it will cost.
