Questions

Before you call.

Do you really come out at night?

Yes. We take emergency enquiries at any time and put particular weight on evenings, overnight, weekends and bank holidays — the times when most plumbers aren't answering. We'll always tell you honestly how soon we can get to you before you agree to anything.

How quickly can you get here?

It depends on where you are, the time and what else is happening that night, so we don't promise a fixed arrival time on the website. Send your postcode and we'll give you a realistic estimate straight away. If we can't get to you in time, we'll say so.

What should I do while I wait?

Turn off the water at the stopcock, open the cold taps to drain the pipes, and keep away from any wet electrics. Our guide to turning off the water in a London flat covers where to look.

How much does an emergency call-out cost?

It depends on the time of day. We tell you the charge before anyone sets off, so there are no surprises. See how our pricing works.

Do you do boiler or gas work?

No. We're an emergency plumbing service: leaks, bursts, blockages and water supply. For anything involving gas, use a Gas Safe registered engineer. If you can smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

Is this a call centre?

No. We're not a national booking line or a directory selling your details. Your message comes to the plumbing team that will attend.
