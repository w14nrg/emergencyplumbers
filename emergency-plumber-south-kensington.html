Stucco houses, lateral flats and mews

SW7 covers the streets around South Kensington station, Gloucester Road, Queen's Gate, Onslow Gardens and Thurloe Square. Most of the large stucco houses here are now lateral flats, one or two per floor, with mews houses behind them and several portered mansion blocks.

  • Lateral flats often have bathrooms stacked directly above each other, so a shower or bath leak comes straight through the ceiling below.
  • Lower-ground flats sit below the street with vaults under the pavement, and can be the first to flood when a drain or stack blocks.
  • Mews houses have compact, boxed-in pipework.
  • The area also has many student lets and short lets, where nobody may know where the stopcock is.

Emergencies we see in South Kensington

We expect bathroom leaks into flats below, blocked stacks backing up into lower-ground flats, burst pipes in boxed-in runs, and leaking cylinders in older flats to be common here. Gloucester Road and Cromwell Road flats are often let or managed, which means we may need to coordinate with an agent.

Getting to you

For portered and managed buildings, tell us who to contact out of hours. For lower-ground flats, tell us about separate area-steps entrances. If you're a guest or tenant and don't know where the stopcock is, send a photo of the kitchen and bathroom pipework and we'll help you look.

Coverage

SW7 is in our core area, which means it's one of the areas we can reach most reliably out of hours. When you contact us we'll give you a realistic arrival time for that night — we don't quote fixed response times on the website because they depend on traffic, time and what else is going on.
