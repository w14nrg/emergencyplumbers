Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Emergency Plumbers London.

Information you send us

When you contact us by WhatsApp or phone, we receive the information you choose to give: your name, number, address or postcode, a description of the problem, and any photos or videos. We use it to respond to your enquiry, attend the job, invoice you and keep a record of the work. We keep job records for as long as we need them for accounting, insurance and legal purposes, and then delete them.

WhatsApp is run by WhatsApp LLC (part of Meta) and messages are subject to its own privacy policy. Phone calls are handled by our phone provider.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we'll update this page first.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes, under its own privacy statement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
