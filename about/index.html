About Emergency Plumbers London

We're an emergency plumbing service based in West London. We do one thing: stop water where it shouldn't be, and fix the cause properly.

Why we exist

Plumbing emergencies rarely happen during office hours. A pipe splits on a Sunday night; a toilet blocks the evening the guests arrive; water comes through the ceiling at 2am. That's when many plumbers aren't answering, and when it's easy to end up with a call centre that sends whoever is free, at whatever price.

Emergency Plumbers London is set up for exactly those hours. We take enquiries at any time, with the emphasis on evenings, overnight, weekends and bank holidays.

How we work

  • WhatsApp first. A photo or short video tells us more than a phone description, so we can judge the problem and bring the right parts.
  • Honest timing. We tell you how soon we can realistically get to you — and if we can't, we say so.
  • Price before we set off. You know the charge for your time of day before you agree to anything.
  • Make safe, then fix. Water off first. Then we find the cause and repair what can be repaired safely on the visit.
  • Plain explanations. If something needs a return visit or a different trade, we explain why.

West and Central London plumbing

We're based in West London and have worked on the buildings here for many years: converted Victorian terraces, red-brick mansion blocks, stucco houses split into lateral flats, mews houses and new riverside developments. Much of what makes emergency plumbing difficult in London is the buildings themselves — shared supplies, stopcocks in odd places, communal tanks and soil stacks serving several flats. Knowing how these buildings are put together is what lets us find and stop leaks quickly.

What we don't do

We don't do gas work, boiler repairs or bathroom refurbishments. We're an emergency plumbing service. If you need a Gas Safe engineer, an electrician or a roofer, we'll tell you.
